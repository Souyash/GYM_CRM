Patented Security Protocol

Stop Pass-Sharing Dead in Its Tracks

Our triple-layer anti-passback engine ensures only paying members enter your facility.

1

GPS Geofenced Radius

The app verifies physical location. Members can only check in if they are physically within your gym's 50-meter GPS boundary. Checking in from home is impossible.

2

Single-Device Binding

A membership account is cryptographically tied to the member's specific smartphone hardware ID. Logging in on a friend's phone triggers an automatic security alert.

3

3-Minute Anti-Tailgate Timer

Once scanned, a cooldown timer prevents the member from scanning again immediately or passing their phone back to an accomplice behind them.
